I work as a Casual Academic where I get the opportunity to teach Computational Theory to students. I get to guide them through rigorous mathematical reasoning, proof techniques, and the exploration of decidability and complexity classes like P and NP. I also introduce alternative models such as quantum computing. Alongside teaching, I mark assignments and provide feedback to help students deepen their understanding and develop strong analytical skills. My role is to support students both in mastering theoretical material and in applying it to solve challenging computational problems.
