I am a contributing author on **Learning Frequency Domain Codes for Semantic Vision**, a research manuscript exploring frequency-domain representations for semantic vision.

My contribution grows from work on frequency-based image segmentation, including a PsychoNet-based decoder and the Fourier Shuffle operation for reconstructing spatial detail from learned frequency information.

The manuscript is currently under revision.
