I supervise capstone teams in DECO3801, where students spend the semester building and shipping a substantial interactive product.

My role is to help teams scope realistically, make sound technical and design decisions, and keep momentum across a long project. I meet with teams regularly to review progress, unpick blockers, and push them towards a build that actually works for its users. I also assess deliverables and give feedback on both the artefact and the process behind it.
